How the numbers were measured

The speech model turns your voice into text on this computer, so the audio does not leave it. Pick one, then download it once; it is saved in this browser. After that, the transcript works with no internet.

Every accuracy and speed number here was measured in this extension. All but Whisper large-v3-turbo ran on the same 113-second reading passage. Turbo's come from a different take: Matt's separate 120-second recording of that passage, on his Mac with WebGPU, on an earlier build; its speed counts from Stop and includes loading the model.

The bars are measured, not guessed: accuracy is how many words a model got right (WER: the share of words wrong); speed is the time it takes as a multiple of the audio length (0.25× audio: 15 s per minute). Point at a bar, or open "Measured", for the details. You can start recording whichever model you pick: Start needs only the live model.

Keep this tab open until a download ends. Then press Use this model to go back.
